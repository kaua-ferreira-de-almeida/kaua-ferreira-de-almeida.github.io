In progress

Vision-guided robotic manipulation

SIGMA Clermont · IMR · S9 · Final-year project

Objective

Integrate perception and robot control in a pick-and-place application. The camera observes the object from the robot arm. The object’s shape and weight will guide the choice between a gripper and a suction cup.

Work completed

  • UR3e control in simulation and Cartesian motion trials with ROS, Gazebo and MoveIt.
  • Initial studies of segmentation models for locating objects to pick.

Next steps

Choose the part and end effector, connect perception to arm control, and test the pick-and-place sequence.
